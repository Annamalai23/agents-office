# Offer ladder

Northgate Studio is a 12-person B2B services studio: websites, brand and monthly growth retainers for trade and professional-services businesses. Revenue is retainers first ([[business-model]]), projects second.

## Packages

### 1. Website build — fixed price, 3 to 5 weeks
**Starter** — $4,800 (one-page website, stock imagery, 3 rounds of revision)
**Standard** — $8,400 (5-page website, custom design, CMS, 5 rounds of revision) ← *recommended*
**Growth** — $14,400 (full site, CMS, SEO setup, analytics, training, 8 rounds of revision)

### 2. Brand kit — $2,400 (logo, colours, type, templates)
**Starter** — $2,400 (logo + 3 colours + 1 font + business card template)
**Standard** — $4,200 (logo + full brand palette + fonts + 3 templates) ← *recommended*
**Growth** — $7,200 (full identity system + social templates + presentation deck)

### 3. Growth retainer — monthly content, ads and reporting
**Starter** — $1,800/mo (4 social posts, 1 newsletter, basic analytics)
**Standard** — $3,600/mo (8 social posts, 2 newsletters, ads management, monthly report) ← *recommended*
**Growth** — $6,000/mo (16 posts, 4 newsletters, ads + SEO, weekly insights, strategy call)

## Pricing rules
- All prices ex-GST unless stated otherwise
- 50% deposit for project-based work; 100% advance for retainers
- Retainers: 30-day minimum, 14-day notice to cancel
- Scope changes re-priced at $120/hr

Proposals follow [[proposal-template]]. Pricing rationale in [[business-model]].
