# Proposal template

> Used by PROPOSALS (piper). Always read offer-ladder.md first.

## Sections

### 1. Your situation
Restate the client's challenge in 2-3 sentences. Show we understand.

### 2. What we will do
Map the deliverables to the offer-ladder package chosen. Three options always; recommend the middle.

### 3. Timeline
| Phase | Duration | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff | Week 1 | Brief confirmed, access granted |
| Build | Weeks 2-3 | First draft |
| Review | Week 4 | Client feedback + revisions |
| Live | Week 5 | Go-live |

### 4. Price
From [[offer-ladder]]. Ex-GST, per month for retainers or fixed price for projects.

### 5. Proof
One relevant case study or example from the Brain.

## Rules
- Subject line under 40 characters, never opens with company name
- CC the owner on the send-ready email
- File final proposal in 60-Sales/proposals/YYYY-MM-DD-client.md
- After writing, hand off to FOLLOW UPS to schedule call within 24 hours
