# Proposal: Acme Corp Website

## Your situation

Acme Corp is a roofing company (45 staff) looking for a modern 5-page website with CMS and monthly SEO. Currently they have an outdated site that generates minimal leads.

## What we will do

**Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy (Week 1)**
- Audit current site
- Define sitemap and user journeys
- Confirm content and assets

**Phase 2: Design (Weeks 2-3)**
- 3 design concepts
- 2 rounds of revisions
- CMS template

**Phase 3: Build (Weeks 4-5)**
- WordPress site build
- 5 pages: Home, Services, About, Blog, Contact
- Contact form + Google Analytics + Search Console
- Mobile-responsive

**Phase 4: SEO & Launch (Week 6)**
- On-page SEO for 20 target keywords
- Monthly SEO retainer begins Month 2

## Options

### Starter — $4,800 (one-page website, stock imagery, 3 rounds of revision)
- 1 page, basic CMS
- Stock imagery only
- 20 target keywords

### Standard (Recommended) — $8,400 (5-page website, custom design, CMS, 5 rounds of revision)
- 5 pages with custom design
- WordPress CMS with training
- SEO for 20 keywords
- Google Analytics + Search Console
- Mobile-responsive

### Growth — $14,400 (full site, CMS, SEO setup, analytics, training, 8 rounds of revision)
- Everything in Standard
- Plus: monthly SEO retainer ($1,200/mo)
- Plus: conversion rate optimization
- Plus: priority support
