# Project plan template

> Used by PROJECT CO-ORDINATOR (pco). Read offer-ladder.md and qa-checklist.md first.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Owner | Duration | Deliverable | QA Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Kickoff | ONBOARDER (ona) | 1-2 days | Brief confirmed, access granted, assets received, kickoff call scheduled | N/A |
| 2. Research | RESEARCH (riley) | 2-3 days | Sources, competitor analysis, content audit | N/A |
| 3. Design round 1 | DESIGNER ASST (dasst) | 3-5 days | Wireframes / mockups v1 | N/A |
| 4. Design sign-off | CLIENT | 2-3 days | Feedback received | N/A |
| 5. Build | GRAPHICS DESIGNER (gfx) / VIDEO EDITOR (vid) | 5-10 days | Working deliverable | N/A |
| 6. Client review | CLIENT | 3-5 days | Client feedback + requested revisions | N/A |
| 7. QA check | QA CHECKER (qa) | 1-2 days | QA report — all checks passed | ✅ Required |
| 8. Client final review | CLIENT | 2-3 days | Final sign-off | N/A |
| 9. Handover | DELIVERY LEAD (dlead) | 1-2 days | Assets delivered, docs complete, training done | N/A |
| 10. Day-7 check-in | ONBOARDER (ona) | Day 7 after handover | Client satisfaction check | N/A |

## Rules
- Hours logged weekly in Notion project board
- Scope changes re-cut the plan the same day
- At-risk milestones flagged to DELIVERY LEAD 48h before deadline
- QA check (step 7) uses [[qa-checklist]] — must pass before client final review
- After handover, immediately hand off to INVOICING for invoice generation
- After delivery, CLIENT REPORTS generates the first monthly report
