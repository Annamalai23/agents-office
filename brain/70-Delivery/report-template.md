# Report template

> Used by CLIENT REPORTS (crep). Read numbers-ledger.md and project-plan-template.md first.

## Sections (4 pages, same order every month)

### 1. What we did
- Completed milestones this month
- Deliverables shipped
- Client feedback received
- Brief narrative (3-4 sentences)

### 2. What happened (numbers, charted)
Pull metrics from [[numbers-ledger]]:
- Traffic (visitors, pageviews)
- Leads generated
- Conversions
- Revenue
- Budget vs actual

### 3. What is next
- Next month's priorities
- Upcoming deliverables
- Client decisions needed
- Risks

### 4. What you need to decide
- 2-3 specific yes/no or choice questions for the client
- Recommended action
- Deadline for response

## Rules
- Four pages max, always the same order
- Numbers must trace to [[numbers-ledger]]
- Sent on the 5th of each month after QA check
- CC the owner
- Hand off to CLIENT EMAILS to send
