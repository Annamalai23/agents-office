# Explorer's Codex

The rules for how this office learns, earns, and keeps going.

## Departments repurposed

The office has six fixed pods. For exploration we use three:

| Pod (department key) | Purpose | Lead agent |
|---|---|---|
| **marketing** | Research & Learning — diving into topics, curating finds, turning them into explainers | LEARNING LEAD (mlead) |
| **delivery** | Synthesis & Output — quest board, fact checking, knowledge vault, templates | OUTPUT role kept, but quest work lives here |
| **ops** | Knowledge Ops — gamification, progress tracking, citation watch | KNOWLEDGE OPS LEAD (olead) |

Emails, Sales and Finance keep their default seats; they are idle for now (an idle agent costs nothing).

## The token loop (earn as you learn)

1. You give the office a quest (a question, topic, or problem to explore).
2. LEARNING CO-ORDINATOR puts it on the Quest Board as **Doing** and assigns it to RESEARCHER.
3. RESEARCHER does the dive, writes a research note, marks it **Done**.
4. FACT CHECKER verifies it → **+10 Knowledge Tokens** to the quest owner.
5. KNOWNGE DIGEST turns the week's notes into a digest → **+5 tokens**.
6. EXPLAINER makes a 90-second explainer → **+3 tokens**.
7. INSIGHT HARVEST pulls a social gem → **+2 tokens**.
8. PROGRESS TRACKER tallies tokens and streaks every Friday.
9. GAMIFICATION ENGINE unlocks badges: Novice (50), Depth Diver (200), Lorekeeper (500), Architect (1000).

## Streak

A streak day counts when at least one piece of learning activity is completed and filed to the Brain. Miss a day, the streak resets.

## Routines (later)

Weekly digest and Friday progress pulse can be scheduled. Only Emails, Finance and Sales get routines in v3.6; we wire those up once we trust the manual loop.
